Hello World!

My name is Lindsey Rogers, and this is my personal website, The Blazing World. The title comes from the 1666 work of the same name by Margaret Cavendish, Duchess of Newcastle-upon-Tyne. I recently read a novel about her and I was genuinely inspired by the way she would just write whatever she was thinking about and publish it. In our current century, is posted online not (almost) as good as published?
